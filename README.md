# PROJECT SUMMARY

# Project Title

Beyond the Likes: Uncovering the Patterns Behind Social Media Content Performance**

# Project Overview

Social media platforms generate enormous amounts of content every day, yet only some posts achieve exceptional audience response. While likes, comments, shares, views, and engagement rates are commonly used to measure performance, these metrics alone do not explain **what makes content successful**.

This project will use Python data analysis techniques to investigate the patterns behind social media content performance. Rather than simply comparing platforms or ranking content types, the study will examine how **audience size, content characteristics, posting behaviour, and platform differences interact with engagement**.

The analysis will ultimately seek to determine whether there are recognizable characteristics that distinguish **high-performing content from ordinary content**.

# Main Objective

To uncover the patterns and characteristics associated with high-performing social media content through exploratory data analysis and statistical investigation using Python.

# Specific Objectives

* To explore how content performance varies across social media platforms and content types.
* To investigate whether audience size is associated with stronger engagement.
* To examine how content characteristics such as category, sentiment, length, hashtags, and media type relate to performance.
* To investigate whether posting time and day are associated with differences in engagement.
* To determine whether the factors associated with strong performance differ across platforms.
* To identify common characteristics of highly engaging content and develop evidence-based insights for improving content performance.

# Research Questions

Rather than treating each variable as a separate topic, the research will follow one central investigation:

### **What patterns distinguish high-performing social media content from ordinary content?**

This central question will be explored through four connected questions:

**1. Audience:**

> **Does having a larger audience necessarily translate into stronger content engagement?**

This will allow us to investigate whether follower count is actually associated with engagement and whether highly followed accounts always produce highly engaging content.

**2. Content:**

> **What characteristics of social media content are most strongly associated with audience engagement?**

This will examine content type, category, sentiment, length, hashtags, and media characteristics.

**3. Timing and Platform:**

> **How do platform and posting behaviour shape the performance of social media content?**

This will investigate whether engagement patterns change according to platform, posting day, and posting hour.

**4. High-Performance Profile:**

> **What characteristics consistently appear among high-performing social media content?**

The findings from the previous questions will be brought together to develop a data-supported profile of highly engaging content.

## Dataset

The project will use a social media engagement dataset containing approximately **5,000 posts and 20 variables** across platforms including Instagram, Twitter, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, and YouTube.

The dataset contains engagement measures such as **likes, comments, shares, views, saves, and engagement rate**, alongside variables describing **followers, content type, category, sentiment, hashtags, content length, verification status, influencer tier, posting time, and platform**.

## Methodology

The project will use **Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn** to:

1. Inspect and understand the dataset.
2. Clean and prepare the data.
3. Perform exploratory data analysis.
4. Analyze relationships between audience, content, timing, platform, and engagement.
5. Use statistical summaries and visualizations to identify meaningful patterns.
6. Compare high-performing and lower-performing content.
7. Interpret the findings and develop evidence-based recommendations.

## Expected Outcome

The project will move beyond simply identifying which platform or content type receives the most likes. It will investigate the **underlying patterns of content performance** and determine whether high-performing posts share identifiable characteristics.

The final analysis will provide insights into the relationship between **who is posting, what they are posting, where and when they are posting, and how audiences respond**.

Ultimately, the project aims to answer:

> ### **“Beyond the likes and views, what actually makes social media content perform?”**
